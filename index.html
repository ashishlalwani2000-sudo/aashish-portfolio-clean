How to Add Your Exact Drawings & Plans

You can easily display your exact architectural floor plans, CAD elevations, and renderings by dropping your image files directly into your GitHub repository!

STEP 1 In GitHub (aashish-portfolio-clean), click Add File → Upload Files.
STEP 2 Upload your images (e.g. ssmc-plan.png, ucsf-axon.jpg, neom-section.png).
STEP 3 Edit index.html and replace placeholder links with /ssmc-plan.png. Vercel will render them live instantly!
